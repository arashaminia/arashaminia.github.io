About Me

I am a mechanical design engineer focused on CAD modelling, mechanical design, simulation and product development.


My work covers the development of mechanical systems from early concepts through detailed 3D modelling, assemblies and engineering documentation.


In addition to CAD and mechanical design, I work with finite element analysis, CFD and multiphysics simulation, using Abaqus and COMSOL Multiphysics to investigate structural and physical behaviour.
