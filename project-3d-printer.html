PROJECT 02 — MACHINE DESIGN

3D PRINTER

Mechanical Design & CAD Development

A mechanical design project focused on the development of a desktop 3D printer, including structural architecture, motion systems, component integration and detailed CAD modelling.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project involved the mechanical development of a 3D printer platform with a focus on structural organisation, mechanical integration, motion systems and overall machine architecture.

The complete concept was developed as a three- dimensional mechanical design, allowing the major components and systems to be considered together as an integrated machine.

03 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Engineering Approach

The design process focused on developing a coherent mechanical system in which the major components could work together as an integrated machine.

01

Mechanical Architecture

Development of the overall machine structure and arrangement of the major mechanical subsystems within the available design space.

02

Motion System

Consideration of the mechanical elements required to provide controlled movement within the printer assembly.

03

Component Integration

Integration of individual mechanical components into a complete assembly while maintaining functional relationships between the subsystems.

04

Design Development

Iterative development of the CAD model to improve the overall machine concept, component arrangement and mechanical organisation.
