PROJECT 02 — MACHINE DESIGN

3D PRINTER

Mechanical Design & CAD Development

Mechanical design and development of a desktop 3D printer, including structural architecture, motion systems, component integration and detailed CAD assembly.

Project Overview

This project involved the mechanical development of a 3D printer platform with a focus on structural rigidity, motion accuracy, component integration and practical mechanical construction.

The complete machine architecture was developed as a 3D CAD assembly, integrating the frame, linear motion components, drive mechanisms, print platform and supporting mechanical hardware.

Engineering Approach

Mechanical Architecture

Motion System

Integration of linear motion components, guides, shafts, drive mechanisms and moving assemblies required for controlled movement.

Structural Design

Focus on frame rigidity, component mounting and mechanical stability to support accurate machine operation.
