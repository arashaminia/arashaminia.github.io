Mechanical Design & CAD Development

I develop mechanical systems from concept to detailed CAD, combining mechanical design with finite element analysis, CFD and multiphysics simulation.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project focuses on the mechanical design and development of a desktop 3D printer.

The design process involved developing the machine architecture, organising the major mechanical components and creating a coherent three-dimensional CAD assembly.

The objective was to create a practical mechanical system with an organised structure and integrated motion components suitable for further engineering development and analysis.

Engineering Approach

My engineering workflow combines mechanical design with numerical simulation and analysis. This allows a design to be evaluated not only geometrically, but also from a performance and engineering perspective.

04 / ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

From Geometry to Performance

I approach engineering design as more than simply creating a 3D model.

My workflow combines mechanical design, CAD development, finite element analysis, CFD and multiphysics simulation to understand how a system can perform before physical prototyping.

This approach allows mechanical concepts to be developed with both geometry and engineering performance in mind.
