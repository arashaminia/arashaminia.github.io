PROJECT 02

3D Printer

Mechanical Design & CAD Development

Mechanical design and development of a desktop 3D printer, including structural architecture, motion systems, component integration and detailed CAD assembly.

Project Overview

This project involved the mechanical development of a 3D printer platform with a focus on structural rigidity, motion accuracy, component integration and practical mechanical construction. The complete machine architecture was developed as a 3D CAD assembly, bringing together the frame, linear motion components, drive mechanisms, print platform and supporting hardware.

Role Mechanical Design Engineer
Primary CAD SolidWorks
Design Focus Mechanical System Development
Analysis FEA & Multiphysics

CAD Development

The machine was developed as a detailed mechanical assembly. The CAD workflow included structural framing, linear guides, drive components, mounting brackets and the integration of multiple mechanical subsystems into a complete machine.

SolidWorks assembly showing the mechanical architecture and integrated motion system.

Engineering Approach

Mechanical Architecture

Development of the overall machine structure and integration of the major mechanical subsystems within a constrained working envelope.

Motion System

CAD development and integration of linear motion, shafts, guides, drive components and moving assemblies required for controlled tool-head movement.

Structural Design

Focus on frame rigidity, component mounting and mechanical stability to support accurate motion during machine operation.

Engineering Simulation

Engineering analysis can be integrated into the development workflow using finite element analysis and multiphysics simulation to evaluate structural and physical behaviour.

Engineering Capability

My work combines mechanical design with engineering simulation. In addition to CAD modelling and product development, I work with finite element analysis, computational fluid dynamics and multiphysics simulation.

My simulation experience includes Abaqus for finite element analysis and COMSOL Multiphysics for coupled multiphysics problems.

This allows me to approach mechanical systems not only from a geometric perspective, but also from structural, thermal, fluid-dynamic and multiphysics perspectives.
