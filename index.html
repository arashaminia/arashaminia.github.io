About Me

My engineering workflow combines digital product development with numerical analysis. I can develop a mechanical concept in CAD, investigate its structural behaviour using finite element analysis, and study fluid, thermal or coupled physical phenomena using CFD and COMSOL Multiphysics.

I am particularly interested in challenging engineering problems where mechanical design and simulation need to work together to create a reliable and practical solution.
