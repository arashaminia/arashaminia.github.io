ABOUT

Mechanical Engineering

I am a mechanical design engineer focused on CAD modelling, mechanical design, product development and engineering simulation.


I enjoy turning engineering concepts into practical, manufacturable designs through detailed 3D modelling, assemblies and technical drawings.


In addition to mechanical design, I work with finite element analysis, CFD and multiphysics simulation, using engineering simulation tools such as Abaqus and COMSOL Multiphysics.


My approach combines mechanical design and simulation to evaluate structural performance, understand engineering behaviour and develop better-performing mechanical systems.

CAD & Design

3D CAD modelling, mechanical assemblies, product development, engineering drawings and design for manufacturing.

FEA & Structural Analysis

Finite element modelling and structural analysis for evaluating mechanical components and systems. Experienced with Abaqus.

CFD & Multiphysics

CFD and multiphysics simulations for understanding fluid flow, thermal behaviour and coupled engineering systems using COMSOL Multiphysics.
