05 / ABOUT

About Me

I am a Mechanical Design Engineer focused on mechanical systems, CAD modelling, product development and engineering simulation.

My approach combines detailed mechanical design with engineering analysis. I enjoy taking a concept, developing its mechanical architecture, building the CAD model and using simulation to understand and improve its behaviour.

My technical interests span mechanical design, machine development, finite element analysis, computational fluid dynamics and multiphysics simulation.

Engineering Philosophy

Good engineering design is more than making a component look right. It should work, withstand its operating conditions, integrate with surrounding systems and be practical to manufacture.
