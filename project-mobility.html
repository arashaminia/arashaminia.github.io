Mobility Vehicle

A compact enclosed mobility concept developed through 3D form development, packaging studies, access analysis and user-oriented design exploration.

Project Overview

This project explores the design of a compact personal mobility vehicle with an enclosed body, integrated seating, four-wheel packaging and a distinctive aerodynamic exterior. The work combines industrial-design form development with mechanical and spatial thinking: component packaging, wheel placement, occupant access, visibility and usable interior space were considered together as the concept evolved.
